# ITAD Jobs

**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Status**: Ready for implementation

> First slice of the app-owned `itad` module. Later specs (assets/intake, data erasure/grading, chain of custody/certificates/dispositions) add entities to the same module and swap the condition sources declared here — they amend the guard registry defined in this document instead of redefining the job lifecycle.

## TLDR

The app-owned `itad` module introduces `ItadJob` — the operational unit of IT asset disposition work that CircularLoop performs for one customer company. A job carries a system-issued `internalReference` (`ITAD-2026-00481`, never reused) and an optional customer-supplied `customerReference` (`NB-RET-1042`), both unique per organization. Its status follows a guarded lifecycle (`draft → scheduled → in_transit → receiving → processing → closeout_review → completed`, plus `on_hold` and `cancelled`): status is never a writable field, every change goes through one transition command that evaluates named conditions and appends an audited history row. The four operational conditions (`receivingComplete`, `allAssetsProcessed`, `noBlockingExceptions` + `requiredDocumentsComplete`) exist from day one with their target names; in this slice they are satisfied by an audited manual confirmation, and later specs replace the source (manifest/assets → exceptions → documents) without changing the state machine. The customer stays owned by the installed `customers` module and is referenced by ID; jobs are managed in their own backend section and in a new "ITAD Jobs" tab on the company detail page.

## Problem Statement

CircularLoop runs ITAD work as B2B jobs for customer companies, but the app has no record for that work. The installed modules offer near-misses only: `CustomerDeal` is a sales opportunity whose status is a pipeline stage and whose fields are value/probability; `WarrantyClaim` is an RMA flow; `SalesOrder` is a commercial document; `wms` has no inbound-receipt record with expected vs. received counts. Forcing jobs into any of them would mix sales and operations reporting and fight their lifecycles.

The job lifecycle also carries compliance weight: a job must not reach `completed` while blocking exceptions or missing documents exist, and every status change must answer "who, when, why". A free-form status field cannot give that guarantee, and the conditions that matter (intake reconciled, all assets processed, exceptions resolved, documents present) depend on records that will only exist after later specs. The lifecycle therefore needs its final shape now, with condition sources that can be upgraded later without rewriting it.

## Overview and Success Measures

- **Primary outcome:** every ITAD job in the PoC lives in one record with a unique internal reference, and 100% of its status changes are recorded with actor, timestamp, and (where required) reason or manual confirmation — zero status changes bypass the transition command.
- **Leading indicators:** jobs created per week; share of transitions that relied on manual confirmation (expected 100% for operational conditions in this slice, falling to 0% as later specs land); number of rejected transitions per condition (shows where the process blocks).
- **Baseline:** `unknown — measurement plan`: there is no job record today; the history table introduced here is the measurement source.
- **Market / product reference:** commercial ITAD ERPs (e.g. Makor ERP, Razor ERP, based on their public product descriptions) model a job/lot with pickup scheduling, receiving against a customer manifest, processing/audit, and closeout with certificates. **Adopted:** manifest-driven receiving as a separate stage, an explicit closeout gate before completion, customer-facing and internal references side by side. **Rejected for the PoC:** financial settlement / rebates, logistics carrier booking, customer portal visibility.

## Goals

- **REQ-001** — Staff can create, view, edit, and (while in `draft`) delete an ITAD job for one customer company, from the ITAD Jobs section.
- **REQ-002** — Every job receives a system-issued `internalReference` `ITAD-{YYYY}-{NNNNN}` from a per-organization counter that restarts every calendar year; a number is never issued twice and never reused.
- **REQ-003** — A job may carry an optional `customerReference`, unique per organization among non-deleted jobs (cancelled jobs keep it reserved; deleting a draft releases it).
- **REQ-004** — Status changes only through the transition command, following the transition table in this spec; invalid transitions and unmet conditions are rejected with a specific, localized reason.
- **REQ-005** — The operational conditions `receivingComplete`, `allAssetsProcessed`, `noBlockingExceptions`, `requiredDocumentsComplete` are declared with their final names and are satisfiable in this slice only by an audited manual confirmation (who, when, comment), gated by a dedicated permission.
- **REQ-006** — `on_hold` stores the pre-hold status, holder, time, and reason; resume returns exactly to the pre-hold status.
- **REQ-007** — Every transition appends an immutable history row (from, to, actor, time, reason, confirmations) visible on the job detail page.
- **REQ-008** — `startedAt` is set by the system on the first entry into `receiving`; `completedAt` on entry into `completed`; neither is user-editable.
- **REQ-009** — Staff can see and start jobs for a company from an "ITAD Jobs" tab on the installed company detail page.

## Non-goals

- `ItadAsset`, manifest items, intake/reconciliation, exceptions, documents, erasure, grading, custody, certificates, dispositions — later specs in the `itad` module.
- `receivedAssetCount` — derived from `ItadAsset` in the assets spec; not stored and not exposed by this slice.
- Person customers (only companies), customer portal access, notifications, scheduled reminders, global search indexing, AI tools.
- Preventing deletion of a customer company that has jobs (recorded as a risk; a mutation guard may follow).
- Financial settlement, quoting, invoicing.

## Proposed Solution

One app module `src/modules/itad/` owns the job record, the reference sequence, the transition history, and the manual condition confirmations. CRUD for the editable fields uses the platform CRUD factory and `CrudForm`/`DataTable`. Status is excluded from CRUD writes; a dedicated command `itad.jobs.transition` (exposed by a custom guarded route) loads the job with its `updatedAt` version, checks the transition table, evaluates every condition the transition requires through an in-module **condition registry**, records manual confirmations, updates status-derived fields, and appends a history row in one transaction; `itad.job.status_changed` is emitted after commit. The UI asks a read endpoint for the transitions currently available to the user and their condition states, so it never re-implements the rules.

### Module Boundary Decision

Decided 2026-10-02: the whole ITAD process lives in one app module `src/modules/itad/` (jobs, assets, data erasure, grading, chain of custody, certificates, dispositions), delivered through several specs.

- **Why one module:** job transition guards must read asset, exception, and document state in the same transaction as the status change; cross-module ORM relations are forbidden, so splitting would force ID/event-only coupling for invariants that must stay transactionally consistent. Chain-of-custody events span every sub-domain of the same asset/job.
- **Internal separation:** sub-domain folders under `commands/`, `api/`, `backend/itad/`, `lib/`; ACL features per sub-domain (`itad.jobs.*`, later `itad.assets.*`, `itad.certificates.*`, …).
- **Outside the module:** customer company (installed `customers`, by ID), users/organizations (`auth`, `directory`), certificate files (`attachments`, later), external erasure/remarketing tools (separate provider packages, later).
- **Spec sequence:** (1) this spec; (2) assets + manifest + intake/reconciliation; (3) data erasure + grading; (4) chain of custody, certificates, dispositions.

### Design Decisions and Alternatives

| Decision | Rationale | Alternative considered | Why rejected / deferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| App-owned `ItadJob` | Lifecycle, references, and guards are ITAD-specific | `CustomerDeal` + custom fields | Status is a sales pipeline stage; mixes sales and ops reporting |
| Own state machine in `lib/job-state-machine.ts` (pattern of installed `warranty_claims/lib/stateMachine.ts`) | Deterministic, testable, transactional with the guard reads | `workflows` module | Not enabled; durable workflow engine is heavier than a 9-state table and cannot share the guard transaction |
| Conditions as an in-module registry with swappable sources | State machine keeps its final shape; later specs replace `manual` with data sources | Hard-coded `true` until later | Loses the audit trail and the target contract |
| Manual confirmations captured inside the transition request | Confirmation and status change are atomic; no orphan confirmations | Separate "confirm condition" action | Confirmations could go stale or exist without a transition |
| Year-reset per-organization counter in its own table, allocated by atomic upsert on the create transaction's connection | Never reused; creates in one organization serialize on one sequence row (row lock held until commit) — acceptable at PoC scale | Postgres `SEQUENCE` per org/year | DDL per tenant/year; harder to scope and test |
| Two distinct concepts: `expectedAssetEstimate` (this slice, optional operator input before the manifest exists) and `expectedAssetCount` (assets spec, derived = number of manifest items, read-only) | No field changes meaning mid-product; the estimate-vs-manifest difference is useful business information (e.g. estimate 500, manifest 487) | One `expectedAssetCount` field, manual now and derived later | Changes the semantics of an existing API field; required `> 0` before `scheduled` was also rejected because pickup can be agreed before the manifest arrives |
| Customer validated and named through the platform `QueryEngine` on entity `customers:customer_entity` (no import of `customers` ORM classes), one batched `id in (…)` query per list page, scoped by tenant + organization | No stale snapshot when a company is renamed; no cross-module ORM coupling; no N+1 | Stored name snapshot | Needs a sync subscriber; not worth it at PoC scale |
| No `itad.jobs.*` command is undoable (`isUndoable: false`) | Undo of create/update/delete would bypass status and editability guards (e.g. delete a `processing` job, restore a taken `customerReference`) | Platform command undo | Corrections use explicit edits and the backward transitions (`scheduled → draft`, `in_transit → scheduled`) |

## Domain Vocabulary and Business Rules

| Term / invariant | Precise meaning or rule | Source of truth | Failure behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| ITAD job | Disposition work for exactly one customer company in one organization | `itad_jobs` | — |
| Customer | A non-deleted `customers:customer_entity` record with `kind = 'company'` in the job's tenant + organization, read through `QueryEngine` (never `customers` ORM classes) | `customers` | Create/update rejected `400 customer_invalid`; list/detail show a "deleted customer" label if the company is deleted later |
| `internalReference` | `ITAD-{YYYY}-{NNNNN}`; `YYYY` = UTC year of the create transaction's timestamp (the same value written to `created_at`); `NNNNN` = per-organization counter for that year, zero-padded to 5 digits, wider when exceeding 99999; assigned at create, immutable | `itad_reference_sequences` + `itad_jobs` | Allocation runs on the create command's own EntityManager transaction; if the create rolls back, the increment rolls back with it |
| Reference reuse | An issued `internalReference` is never issued again, including after cancellation or soft delete | unique index without `deleted_at` filter | DB unique violation → `409 reference_conflict` (must never happen; signals a sequence bug) |
| Reference ordering | Lists sort "by reference" using `created_at`, never the reference string (widths can differ) | list route | — |
| `customerReference` | Optional, trimmed, 1–64 chars; unique per organization case-insensitively among jobs where `deleted_at is null` (cancelled jobs included) | partial unique index on `lower(customer_reference)` | `409 customer_reference_taken` |
| Status | One of the 9 statuses below; written only by `itad.jobs.transition` | `itad_jobs.status` | `status` (or any hold/system field) in a CRUD body → `400 field_not_writable` |
| Terminal | `completed`, `cancelled`: no transitions out, all fields read-only, not deletable | state machine | `409 terminal` |
| Delete | Allowed only in `draft`; soft delete; releases `customerReference` | delete command | `409 delete_not_draft` for every non-draft job, terminal included |
| `startedAt` | Set to the transition time on the first entry into `receiving` only; later re-entries (hold → resume, any future path back) never change it | transition command | read-only |
| `completedAt` | Set on entry into `completed` | transition command | read-only |
| Hold | Entering `on_hold` stores `statusBeforeHold`, `heldAt`, `heldBy`, `holdReason`; resume sets status = `statusBeforeHold` and clears the four fields (history keeps them) | `itad_jobs` + history | — |
| Manual confirmation | A user with `itad.jobs.confirm_conditions` asserts a manual-source condition is met, with a required comment (3–1000 chars); stored with user and time, linked to the transition it enabled | `itad_job_condition_confirmations` | Missing feature → `403`; missing comment → `400` |

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Terminal |
|---|---|---|
| `draft` | Created; data still being completed | no |
| `scheduled` | Pickup / handover datetime agreed | no |
| `in_transit` | Equipment travelling to the ITAD facility | no |
| `receiving` | Physical intake, scanning, reconciliation against the manifest | no |
| `processing` | Devices being processed: sanitization, exceptions | no |
| `closeout_review` | Operations finished; completeness of data and documents checked | no |
| `completed` | Closed; reports and certificates ready | yes |
| `on_hold` | Temporarily paused; resumes to the pre-hold status | no |
| `cancelled` | Abandoned | yes |

### Transition table

| Action ID | From | To | Required conditions | Reason required | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `schedule` | `draft` | `scheduled` | `schedulingDataComplete` | no | — |
| `unschedule` | `scheduled` | `draft` | — | yes | — |
| `dispatch` | `scheduled` | `in_transit` | — | no | — |
| `return_to_scheduled` | `in_transit` | `scheduled` | — | yes | — |
| `start_receiving` | `in_transit` | `receiving` | — | no | `startedAt` if null |
| `start_processing` | `receiving` | `processing` | `receivingComplete` | no | — |
| `start_closeout` | `processing` | `closeout_review` | `allAssetsProcessed` | no | — |
| `complete` | `closeout_review` | `completed` | `noBlockingExceptions`, `requiredDocumentsComplete` | no | `completedAt` |
| `hold` | `scheduled`, `in_transit`, `receiving`, `processing`, `closeout_review` | `on_hold` | — | yes | hold fields set |
| `resume` | `on_hold` | `statusBeforeHold` | — | no | hold fields cleared |
| `cancel` | any non-terminal (incl. `draft`, `on_hold`) | `cancelled` | — | yes | hold fields cleared |

A reason is 3–1000 characters, stored on the history row (and as `holdReason` for `hold`).

### Field editability by status

Editable through `PUT /api/itad/jobs` (fields not listed are never writable through CRUD: `status`, `internalReference`, hold fields, `startedAt`, `completedAt`). `on_hold` follows the column of its `statusBeforeHold`.

| Field | `draft` | `scheduled` | `in_transit` | `receiving` | `processing` | `closeout_review` | `completed` / `cancelled` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `customerId` | ✓ | — | — | — | — | — | — |
| `name` | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| `customerReference` (set/change/clear) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| `scheduledPickupAt` | ✓ (clearable) | ✓ (not clearable) | — | — | — | — | — |
| `expectedAssetEstimate` | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — | — | — | — |

After `dispatch` the planned pickup becomes part of the job's history: `scheduledPickupAt` is locked from `in_transit` on (a backward move `in_transit → scheduled` makes it editable again). Correcting a historical pickup date later requires a dedicated audited action (out of scope). From `receiving` on, the estimate is read-only because manifest/asset data take over.

Writing a non-editable field → `400 field_locked` with the field name (or `409 terminal` for terminal jobs). The `CrudForm` renders locked fields read-only using the same matrix returned by the API (`editableFields` in the detail response), so UI and server cannot drift.

### Conditions

| Condition | Meaning | Source in this slice | Planned source (later spec) | Manually confirmable now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `schedulingDataComplete` | Customer valid, `name` set, `scheduledPickupAt` set | data (job fields + customer lookup) | unchanged | no |
| `receivingComplete` | Intake and reconciliation against the manifest finished | manual | manifest / assets (spec 2) | yes |
| `allAssetsProcessed` | Every asset has finished its processing path | manual | assets (spec 2/3) | yes |
| `noBlockingExceptions` | 0 open blocking exceptions | manual | exceptions (spec 3) | yes |
| `requiredDocumentsComplete` | All required documents/certificates exist | manual | documents (spec 4) | yes |

Condition registry contract (`lib/job-conditions.ts`, module-internal): each condition declares `key`, `source` (`data` | `manual`), `manualAllowed: boolean`, and `evaluate(job, ctx) → met | unmet (with localized detail) | confirmation_required`. In this slice the four operational conditions are `source: 'manual', manualAllowed: true` and evaluate to `confirmation_required`; `schedulingDataComplete` is `source: 'data', manualAllowed: false`. Later specs switch a condition to `source: 'data'` and set `manualAllowed: false`; confirmations already stored remain as history.

A transition succeeds only when every required condition is `met`, or is `confirmation_required` and the request carries a valid confirmation for it. Confirmation payload validation (all `400`):

- `confirmation_duplicate` — the same `condition` appears more than once;
- `confirmation_not_required` — the condition is not required by this action, or already evaluates to `met`;
- `confirmation_not_allowed` — the condition has `manualAllowed: false`;
- `comment_required` — comment missing or outside 3–1000 chars.

### Error catalog

All error codes are prefixed `itad.jobs.errors.` (translation keys and `code` in the response body). When several apply, the first row that matches wins.

| Precedence | Code | HTTP | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `organization_required` | 400 | no organization selected in scope |
| 2 | (platform) | 401 / 403 | not authenticated / missing feature (`confirm_conditions` when confirmations are sent) |
| 3 | (platform) | 404 | job not found in scope |
| 4 | (platform optimistic lock) | 409 | expected version is stale |
| 5 | `delete_not_draft` | 409 | **DELETE only:** any non-`draft` job, terminal included (DELETE never returns `terminal`) |
| 6 | `terminal` | 409 | **PUT / transition only:** job is `completed`/`cancelled` |
| 7 | `transition_not_allowed` | 400 | action not valid from the current status |
| 8 | `field_not_writable` | 400 | system field in a CRUD body (POST or PUT) |
| 9 | `field_locked` | 400 | editable-by-matrix rule violated (incl. `customerId` after `draft`, clearing `scheduledPickupAt` in `scheduled`, any `scheduledPickupAt` change from `in_transit` on, `expectedAssetEstimate` from `receiving` on) |
| 10 | validation codes: `reason_required`, `comment_required`, `confirmation_duplicate`, `confirmation_not_required`, `confirmation_not_allowed`, `customer_invalid`, zod field errors | 400 | input invalid |
| 11 | `condition_unmet` (with `conditions: [key…]`) | 400 | required condition unmet and not confirmed |
| 12 | `customer_reference_taken` / `reference_conflict` | 409 | unique violation |

**History vs. audit log — responsibility split:**

- `ItadJobStatusTransition` (+ `ItadJobConditionConfirmation`) is the **domain source of truth** for the job lifecycle and is what operators see. It answers "when and why did this job move from Receiving to Processing, and who confirmed what?"
- `audit_logs` (platform, via the command bus) is the **technical record of command execution** for system audit. It answers "which command did a user run and what changed in the system?"
- Business history is never reconstructed from `audit_logs`, and lifecycle UI never reads it.

## User Journeys

### Journey J-001 — Create and schedule a job

1. Operator opens **ITAD → Jobs** and clicks **New job**.
2. Fills customer (picker), name, optional customer reference, optional expected asset estimate, optional scheduled pickup datetime; saves. The job is created in `draft` with `ITAD-2026-00042`; the user lands on its detail page.
3. On the detail page the operator clicks **Schedule**. If `scheduledPickupAt` is missing, the dialog lists the unmet condition with a link to edit; otherwise the job moves to `scheduled` and the history shows the row.
4. Duplicate customer reference → inline field error on save, input preserved. Another user edited the job meanwhile → conflict message with reload.

### Journey J-002 — Operate through the happy path with manual confirmations

1. Operator moves `scheduled → in_transit → receiving` (`startedAt` set).
2. **Start processing** requires `receivingComplete`. An operator without `itad.jobs.confirm_conditions` sees the condition as "requires supervisor confirmation" and the action disabled with that explanation.
3. A supervisor opens the same dialog, ticks **Confirm manually**, writes a comment, submits (Cmd/Ctrl+Enter). The job moves to `processing`; history shows the transition and the confirmation (who, when, comment).
4. Same for `start_closeout` (`allAssetsProcessed`) and `complete` (two confirmations). `completedAt` is set; the job becomes read-only.

### Journey J-003 — Hold, resume, cancel, correct

1. From `receiving` an operator clicks **Put on hold**, enters a reason. Status `on_hold`; the detail header shows "On hold since … by … — reason" and "was: Receiving".
2. **Resume** returns to `receiving`.
3. A failed pickup: from `in_transit`, **Back to scheduled** with reason.
4. **Cancel** from any non-terminal status with reason, confirmed in a destructive confirmation dialog.

### Journey J-004 — Customer context

1. A user opens a company in **Customers → Companies**, tab **ITAD Jobs**.
2. Sees the company's jobs (references, name, status, dates), opens one, or clicks **New job** — the create form opens with the customer preselected.

```text
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

```

Transition dialog:

```text

│ Conditions                                           │
│  ◻ Receiving complete — requires confirmation        │
│     [✓] Confirm manually   Comment: [___________]    │
│ Reason (only for actions that require one): [_____]  │
│                               [Cancel]  [Confirm]    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Behavior:** the status action menu lists only actions returned by `GET …/transitions`; actions whose conditions cannot be satisfied by the current user are shown disabled with the localized reason. Submitting sends the job `updatedAt`; `409` shows the shared conflict message and reloads. Cancel uses `useConfirmDialog` (destructive). Delete only in `draft`.
- **Responsive and accessibility:** dialog fields labelled; condition states conveyed by text + icon, not color alone; history is a semantic list.
- **Localization:** namespace `itad.*` in `src/modules/itad/i18n/{en,pl,de,es}.json` (all locales the app ships); status labels `itad.jobs.status.<value>`, actions `itad.jobs.actions.<id>`, conditions `itad.jobs.conditions.<key>`, errors `itad.jobs.errors.*`.
- **Design-system and theming:** `StatusBadge` with semantic variants per status (no hard-coded colors), semantic tokens, verified in light and dark mode.

| `GET` | `/api/itad/jobs/{id}/history` | `itad.jobs.view` | — | `{ items: [{ action, from, to, reason, actor: { id, name }, createdAt, confirmations: [...] }] }` | 404 | REQ-007 |

- CRUD routes use `makeCrudRoute` with the `itad.jobs.*` commands; transition and history routes are custom routes with per-method `metadata` (`requireAuth`, `requireFeatures`) and `openApi`.
- Scope from request context only; every query filters `tenant_id` + `organization_id`; cross-org IDs return 404.
- Optimistic locking: the platform opt-in mechanism — the client sends the expected `updatedAt` in the `x-om-ext-optimistic-lock-expected-updated-at` header (`CrudForm` does this automatically for update/delete; the transition dialog sends it through the shared scoped-header helper). `makeCrudRoute` auto-registers the generic lock reader for `ItadJob`; the custom transition route checks the same header inside the command. Mismatch → 409 surfaced by `CrudForm` / the shared conflict helper. Implementation step 1 verifies the versioned delete path through the factory; if it is not covered, the delete command performs the same check.
- Idempotency: a retried transition with a stale version gets 409, never a double transition.
- Undo: every `itad.jobs.*` command declares `isUndoable: false` (see Design Decisions).

## Security, Privacy, and Compliance

- **Authorization:** feature gates only (`itad.jobs.view|manage|transition|confirm_conditions`), never role names. Manual confirmation requires its own feature so it can be restricted to supervisors.
- **Tenant isolation:** all reads/writes scoped by trusted tenant + organization; customer validation looks up the company within the same scope, so a foreign company ID is rejected as invalid (no existence leak).
- **Sensitive data:** none classified; reasons/comments are free text — UI hint discourages personal data.
- **Abuse and failure modes:** status cannot be set via CRUD; transition replay blocked by `updatedAt`; references are server-generated (no client control); history and confirmations are append-only with no API to modify them.

## Risks and Tradeoffs

| Risk / tradeoff | Impact | Mitigation / detection | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer company deleted while jobs exist | Job shows "deleted customer"; reporting gaps | read-time lookup tolerates missing; listed as non-goal | Accepted for PoC; mutation guard on company delete can follow |
| UTC year boundary | A job created between 00:00 and 01:00 Polish time (CET, UTC+1) on 1 January gets the previous year in its reference | documented rule; reference year derived from the same UTC timestamp as `created_at` | Accepted for PoC (decided 2026-10-02) |
| Manual confirmations overused after data sources exist | Guards weakened | per-condition `manualAllowed` flag flips off in later specs; indicator metric | Low |
| Concurrent transitions | Double transition | `updatedAt` lock → 409 | None |
| Sequence row contention under heavy concurrent creation | Slower creates | single-row upsert is short; acceptable at PoC scale | Low |
| Estimate differs from manifest | Operators may confuse estimate and declared count | separate labels ("Estimated assets" vs "Manifest assets" in spec 2); estimate stays editable only until `receiving` | Low |
